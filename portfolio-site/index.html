Background

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Drexel pursuing a combined BS/MS with a minor in Computer Science, on the 5-year co-op track. My coursework covers controls, dynamics, CAD, and CAE, with performance enhancement of dynamic systems and advanced dynamics in progress.

Most recently I was a Control Systems Co-op at Thermo Systems, programming PLC ladder logic, designing SCADA interfaces, and supporting on-site testing and commissioning. I'm also President of ASME at Drexel, after serving as vice president and as a general executive board member. For my next co-op, I want to explore a new side of engineering, like robotics or product design, and keep learning by getting my hands dirty. Whether I'm designing, building, or troubleshooting, I enjoy seeing how my work translates into something tangible.
